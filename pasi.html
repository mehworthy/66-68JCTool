Tap a body region (or its tile), then score area and severity, counting front and back together. Each region fills from the bottom by area, coloured by erythema, with a dark edge for induration and white flecks for desquamation that fill the affected area from the bottom, a quarter per point. Each label shows the region score, then its area and sign scores.
